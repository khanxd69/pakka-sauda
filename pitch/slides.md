## 1. Problem: Rashid's harvest

- Rashid, a potato farmer in Okara, has about 10,000 kg ready and one buyer at the farm gate.
- The price is agreed by phone. When the truck arrives, the grade "drops" and so does the money.
- His arhti also gives him credit, so the arhti is not the villain; the deal simply has no locked terms.
- **Fact:** arhtis provide credit and charge a 2–4% commission (PIDE Knowledge Brief 2022:69).

*Speaker note:* tell it as one person's story; don't generalise beyond the source.

---

## 2. Why listings aren't enough

- Finding a buyer is not the hard part. The deal breaks **at the farm gate**: over grade, over weight, or because the buyer doesn't show.
- What's missing is terms that are fixed **before** anyone sees the crop, and a rule for what happens when they disagree.
- *Never claim another platform failed.*

---

## 3. Live demo (≈ 2.5 min)

1. Rashid lists by Urdu voice: 250 mann, next week, grade A, Rs 1,520 per mann. Every number is read back.
2. Buyers bid a **price per grade**: Imran A 45 / B 39 / C not accepted (Rs/kg).
3. Rashid hears the offer and "what reaches your hand" vs the mandi → accepts. Token paid (DEMO).
4. Collection: 9,200 kg grade B → Rs 3,58,800 by rule: **kg × the agreed price for that grade**.
5. Dispute: a seeded deal recorded as C; Rashid disputes; a human reviewer rules B: Rs 2,40,000 → Rs 3,12,000.

## 4. Business model

- **1% fee from the buyer on settled deals.** The farmer pays nothing.
- Revenue is counted only when the farmer confirms he was paid (settled).
- First market: Okara potatoes → Lahore wholesale buyers.
- *Only quote a market size you can source.* (No market-size figure in this deck.)

## Judges' Q&A (short answers)

- *Different from AgriConnect / Kissan Market?* They help you find a buyer; we lock the terms and settle them at the farm gate by rule.
- *Why leave the arhti?* We don't ask him to; we're one more buyer channel.
- *Bypass after the first deal?* The protection (token, ladder, dispute review, record) only exists on the platform.
- *Urdu speech is unreliable.* Every number is read back and confirmed; tapping always works.
- *Fake photo?* Live camera, one-time code, time and location stamps; nothing is loaded until the farmer agrees.
- *Who holds the money?* Nobody, in the demo. A pilot needs a licensed payment partner and legal review.
- *Where's the AI?* It listens and explains in Urdu. It deliberately doesn't decide who gets paid.
